# APIP TimeBank — Decisions and Open Questions

## Confirmed project direction
- Browser extension installation is free.
- Intended funding model is hybrid: subscription revenue and other verified business income may fund rewards.
- The MVP uses synthetic data and has no real payment processing.
- StakeandWager.com is the designated intended IP owner, subject to legal verification.

## Unresolved before commercial launch
- Subscription price and billing cadence.
- Reward allocation percentage or policy.
- Approved operating deductions and reserve levels.
- Eligible activities and evidence standards.
- Reward period, dispute window, rounding and residual policy.
- Target jurisdictions, currencies, tax treatment and payment methods.
- Legal classification, ownership assignments, privacy review and launch approvals.

Do not infer missing decisions from code defaults. Version every policy used for a calculation.

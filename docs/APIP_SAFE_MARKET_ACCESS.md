# APIP safe market access — capability and privacy rules

## Current status

APIP can research public opportunity listings and prepare offers locally. This Codex task has web search but no connected freelancer marketplace, APIP-owned publishing account, email, or CRM. No Upwork connector surfaced; candidate Canva/WordPress/Metricool and email/CRM apps were not installed. No account was connected, no recipient list was available, and no external message or offer was sent.

## Allowed independent work

- Discover public buyer postings and public business needs; preserve URLs, observation date, visible budget, eligibility, and activity signals.
- Draft a scoped offer, sample, and proposal. Label estimates and do not present listings or replies as guaranteed demand.
- Prepare a send queue only when a genuine, relevant public opportunity and authorized account are available.

## Outbound gates

Before any message or publication, require an installed and authenticated account whose owner and permitted actions are clear; use the platform's supported workflow and terms. Match a real offer to a relevant recipient/opportunity, cap the experiment to the owner's authorization, avoid bulk or repeated unsolicited sends, honor platform rules and opt-outs, and stop on complaint or negative consent. Never bypass authentication, access restrictions, rate limits, or platform contact rules.

## Secret and data boundaries

- Never collect, store, transmit, or publish bank account details, passwords, PINs, OTPs, API keys, private financial records, or unrelated personal data for marketing.
- Use only the minimum business information needed to qualify a lead. Do not enrich personal contact details unless the user and applicable rules explicitly permit that use.
- Keep credentials in the connector's secure authorization flow; never put tokens in prompts, source files, campaign logs, or generated samples.
- Separate public research, private customer replies, and financial evidence. Redact secrets before writing audit events. Retain only the minimum business outcome, opt-out, or complaint evidence APIP needs.
- Draft by default; where the existing owner authorization explicitly covers one scoped experiment and a connected account supports it, APIP may execute only that experiment within the authorized boundaries and record its evidence.

## What counts as progress

Research is not contact. A draft is not a sent offer. A sent proposal is not a hire. A hire is not delivered work. Delivery is not revenue. Record revenue only when actual payment is supported by independent payment evidence; keep gross contract value, platform fees, refunds, other costs, and net amount distinct.

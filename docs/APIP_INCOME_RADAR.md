## Campaigning and payment

- APIP may prepare service samples, campaign plans, and tailored drafts autonomously.
- The previously authorized cap is at most one small APIP marketing/customer-discovery experiment if a relevant APIP-owned account and recipient basis exist. No account is currently connected. Do not send or publish until that capability is actually available; obey platform terms, opt-outs, complaints, and data minimization.

- A direct foreign client may propose Western Union only where the sending/receiving countries, commercial purpose, payment route, and terms permit it. Western Union is a transfer service, not an escrow or buyer-acquisition system. Confirm the funds have settled in the recipient's own account before recording payment.

The account holder enters bank details only in a trusted provider's secure settings or gives minimum necessary information directly to a verified payer through a suitable private channel.

## Explicit exclusions

“Mining” means discovering/qualifying paid opportunities in this APIP workflow. It does not mean installing cryptocurrency mining software, trading crypto or tokenized oil/agricultural assets, or claiming speculative return as income. Commodity trading, token investment, and paid mining are outside the current authorization and are not a substitute for customer revenue.

## Measurement

Daily APIP performance is **verified money received minus fees, refunds, and attributable expenses**, supported by a platform transaction statement, payout receipt, and/or bank evidence. Record proposed value, signed contract value, delivered value, gross payment, fees, and net receipt separately. Report the NGN 10,000/day target only as achieved on days for which independent payment evidence supports net receipts of at least NGN 10,000. Historical daily earnings remain unestablished until source records are reconciled.

# APIP Protocol Setup — Demo Deliverable

**Status: DEMO ONLY. No customer research, outreach, sale, or payment is represented here.**

## What a buyer receives
- A simple experiment ledger template.
- An evidence register that separates claims from proof.
- A validation plan with a clear pass/fail/ambiguous outcome.
- A one-page report format that keeps revenue and costs separate from interest.

## Intended customer
Solo builders and small teams who run small product or service experiments and need to track what they tested, what people did, what it cost, and what money actually arrived.

## The promise (to test, not assume)
"Run one small income experiment with a clear hypothesis, a traceable evidence trail, and an honest financial result."

## Rules
1. Hypothesis is not demand.
2. Compliments are not purchase intent.
3. Purchase intent is not payment.
4. A payment is not net profit; subtract fees, refunds, and direct costs.
5. A blank or unknown value stays blank/unknown. Never invent evidence.
6. Do not contact people, publish offers, or spend money without the owner's explicit approval.

## Files
- `experiment-ledger-template.csv`: one row per experiment; empty template.
- `evidence-register-template.csv`: evidence and provenance register.
- `validation-plan.md`: zero-cost, owner-approved validation procedure.
- `validation-report-template.md`: report format after evidence exists.
- `example-record-DEMO-ONLY.csv`: illustrative example; fictional and not a real result.

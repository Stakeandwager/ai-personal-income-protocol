# Zero-cost validation plan (requires owner approval before any outreach)

## Objective
Find out whether the target user has a real tracking problem and whether the proposed setup service is useful enough to justify a paid pilot.

## Hypothesis
At least 3 of 5 relevant solo builders/small teams report that they currently lose track of experiment outcomes, evidence, or net receipts, and at least 1 asks to see or try the sample without being prompted to praise it.

This is an initial decision rule, not a statistically representative market estimate.

## Steps
1. Inspect the sample package yourself and record any usability problems.
2. Draft a short, neutral request for feedback. Do not claim customers, results, or income.
3. Get explicit owner approval for the exact message, recipients/channel, and timing before contacting anyone.
4. Ask about their current workflow first; show the demo only after listening.
5. Record each response as an observation, not as a sale. Keep personal data minimal and do not publish names or quotes without permission.
6. Ask whether they would use it, what is missing, and whether they would pay for a defined setup. Do not lead them toward “yes.”
7. Record evidence references and classify the result below.

## Outcome rules
- `TESTED_DEMAND`: the predeclared threshold is met with traceable evidence. This means demand signal only, not income.
- `TESTED_NO_DEMAND`: the test was completed and the threshold was not met.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: too few responses, unclear feedback, or evidence cannot support a conclusion. Do not treat as terminal success.
- `NOT_RUN`: no validation contact or test has occurred.

## Financial rules
- Before payment: income received = 0.
- If a customer pays, record gross amount, fees, refunds, direct costs, net received, date, and proof reference separately.
- Never count a promise, invoice, verbal interest, or unpaid amount as cash received.
- Spend ceiling for this test: NGN 0 unless a specific expense is separately approved.
